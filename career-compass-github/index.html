How it works

STEP 1

Tell us about you

Studies, 27 skills rated 0 to 4, interests, projects and goals.

STEP 2

Check your answers

A 3-question check per skill and an 18-item interest test.

STEP 3

Transparent scoring

Seven weighted factors score each of 13 careers.

STEP 4

Get your plan

Matches, skill gap, roadmap, projects and readiness.

Why you can trust the result

Every score is explained

Open any career to see each factor's score, weight and points. Nothing is hidden.

Skills are verified

A skill counts at most one level above the number of quiz questions you answer correctly.

Accuracy is measured

Counsellors rate recommendations, and the results are tracked with standard measures.
